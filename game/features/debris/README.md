The debris drifting past the ship, drawn: the sim (`sim/Debris`) spawns and hands out what a catch gives.
Catching is the hero's hands, aimed as everything E works is (`features/crew`, `Aiming.AimAt`), sending `Grab`;
breaking wreckage with a weapon is the hands too, sending `Hit` (0034, 0054).

- **Reads:** `DebrisModule.Pieces` and `Found`, `Held`; `Client.InFlight` (own grabs, `Shared.Predicted.Grabbing`, and hits,
  `OwnHits`); `Ui.AimedPiece`; `Ui.Heroes`; `Ui.Bits`; `Session.Drift`.

## Rules

- **Only what the camera sees** (0052): a piece gets a picture only while its middle is within `ShipCamera.View` and
  8 cells round it. Pictures come from two pools, wrecks' and small pieces', that grow to the most ever on screen and
  never free, warmed in `_Ready`, so a cloud drifting in makes
  none. Only wrecks take the cracking material; a small piece is `Lit` plain. A piece leaving the view goes back to its pool.
- **Drawn** behind the back walls (`Layers.Debris`): it never collides with the hull. Each piece wears one of
  its kind's pictures (`Content/DebrisLooks`, `art/debris`), the one its id picks (`Content.Looks.Of(kind, id)`), so
  every peer sees it alike (0066); a wreck's is drawn of what it breaks into and is the box it is aimed at and struck
  by (`DebrisLooks.Wrecks`). A kind with none is its item's icon at 0.8 of a cell. Lit by the stars and lamps; drawn
  where `Shared.PieceDrawn` says: the host player's body (`LiveDebris`), else the sim's pose run on, a guest easing
  each correction away (`PieceEase`, debris-coop).
- **Bodies** (debris-host-physics): on the host player's client a body a ship's piece wherever its hero is, meeting only pieces,
  eased to its drift and tumble; guests' lines too; lines pull by `PieceRope`; bursts push (`BurstPush`, debris-bursts); strays past `FeedSlack` go to the sim as `Moved`.
- **Damage** (cracks-to-end): a dent per blow seen, one within 3 px deepening it, and one network out of the first
  (`game/shared`'s `WreckCracks`, `WreckInk` on the picture, lit, clipped) run on with the share lost to cover it by 0.92; one first
  seen damaged is struck in its middle. Kept out of view; blows there go unseen.
- **A hit** (a rise in `Damage`) jolts the piece away from the nearest hero, turning it a little and settling, and
  throws `HitLooks.Of[Impact.Wreck]` by `HitBurst` where it was struck, the picture flaring warm (0075, 0094), facing
  the nearest hero; none where a haze lay in it (`Ui.HazeTouched`). One under a running tool's bit (`Ui.Bits`,
  within 4 px of the box) only cracks it: the crew shows the tool at work there all along (tool-contact).
- **Own hits** (`OwnHits`, read after this frame's hits are sent) are felt the frame they are first in flight (0014):
  the jolt, throw and flare, the view nudged (`ShipCamera.Nudge`) and `Sound.Chop`, on the Suit bus, dulled while
  floating (0042); their tick brings only the cracks.
- **Broken** (`Fading.Broken`): a wreck gone before the end of its path, not caught by hand (`CaughtFrom`), counting ticks since last
  seen, so one drifting off its end only fades, as does one broken past its end while a hero's view keeps it there
  (0052), its loot falling all the same. It flies apart in six tumbling wedges with glowing torn edges and
  embers (`BreakView`), with sparks, dust, a cloud, glint, ring and flash, more for a derelict (0075); `Sound.Break`
  if its own hit broke it, louder and farther, on the Suit bus. Every peer sees it; only the striker hears it, after the last hit's chop.
- **Aimed** (0039, 0054): the piece in `Ui.AimedPiece` (in space, within reach; never with a tool or weapon
  in hand, melee-edge) is outlined along its picture a screen pixel wide (`render/Outline.cs`), green, or red while the
  sim would refuse the grab. The crew's prompt says "[E] Catch", on a wreck "Too big".
- **Own grabs at once** (0014): a piece with a grab of this peer's in flight is gone from its view and skipped by the
  aim, so E cannot send it twice.
- **Deliveries** (mafia-deliveries): an amber beacon blinks on each as on a body, lit: on a wreck's top (the
  crate's lamp), a parcel's middle. What one gives shows as loot (`GoodIcons`).
- **Nets** (2026-11-03-debris-net): the grid draws the booms (`machine_net`); `NetView` the plates on the hull's face, then
  each held piece in its own look at its place by load (`NetShows.Place`), then `net_front_<held>` sagging over them. Read
  every 100 ms; a catch pops in. E on a net sends `Collect` (`Aiming`), through its wall too; its tip says "7/10". What a
  collect gave shows as a grab's (items' `OwnCatches`), flying from where `NetShows.Lying` says each piece lay.

## The Handbook's Debris section (0066, 0083)

- `HandbookPage` on `features/handbook`'s tablet: every kind in rows by where to look (`Content/Handbook.DebrisRows`). A found kind
  shows its first look found; one not found its first look black.
- **Tally** at the tab strip's right: looks found of all there are, with a bar.
- **Entry** of the chosen kind (the first found until one is clicked): the look big, a strip of every look (not found
  black, a click shows a found one big, "2/5"; none for a one-look kind); name, hit points (`Wreckage.HpOf`) or
  "Caught by hand"; loot slot by slot (`Loot.Of`): a seen item with its range, an unseen one a black silhouette, a pie
  for a roll's chance (`OneIn`).
- Words only in the one tooltip (`Finds`); "???" for what is not found or seen.
- Reads `Found` every 250 ms while shown, rebuilt on a change.

## Deliberately not handled

- Other peers' grabs in flight: a piece another player is catching stays until the host's tick says so.
- Own hits before their tick (0014): a chop jolts and is heard a round trip after the swing; its pace hides it.
- Which hero struck: the jolt faces the nearest.
- Off the view: a hit on a piece not drawn jolts nothing; a wreck broken there bursts unseen.
