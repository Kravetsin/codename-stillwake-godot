# logistics (client): the drone badge and drones on screen

Drones ([0062](../../../docs/decisions/0062-drones-keep-machines-going.md)), the client of `sim/Logistics`.
It reads `LogisticsModule` (`Served`, `Servable`, `Reached`, `Drones`, `Low`, `ZoneOf`), `IItemsQueries` (`Crate`),
`IGridQueries` (`MachinesWith(Zone)`: the bays, 0113; `MachineById`, `StructureAt`), `IPowerQueries` (`NodeAt`,
`NetworkOf`, `Charges`) and `IAtmosphereQueries` (`AiredAt`), and changes the sim only by sending `SetServed`. Its badge lives in other features' frames,
wired by `Main`: the header of every machine frame (`InventoryPanel.Served`) and a queued station's
(`FabricatorPanel.Served`), where drones have work. The bay's slots, charge bars and frame bolt are the items
feature's. Picks: drone A, motion "lively" (`DroneLook.cs`); the bay «Хребет с кронштейнами» and the salvage drone
«Сетчатая рама» ([salvage drones](../../../docs/decisions/2026-11-03-salvage-drones.md); `SalvageLook.cs`).

## Guarantees

- **The badge** (0081): a wrist key, its face a drone, right of the header. Lit while drones serve the machine and a
  bay reaches it; dim and crossed while switched off; grey where no bay reaches. Shown only where drones ever have
  work (`Servable`). A click sends `SetServed`; the tooltip says the state and what a click does. Read every 100 ms
  and at once after its own command, showing its own switch before its tick (0014).
- **A remembered input** (`KeptAt`, while served; `InventoryPanel.Kept`): a small sky dot in its top-left; empty, a
  faint ghost of the item. Its tooltip, empty, says "Drones keep this full: X", with "none in reach" in red when no
  crate or output a bay reaches holds it (`Supplied`). `SlotBox.Kept` draws both.
- **The bay's frame:** four drone slots, each a drone's faint silhouette while empty, a gap, then the fuel slot,
  a canister's ghost (`SlotMark.Breathes`, tooltip `logistics.fuel`); a docked drone's slot shows its charge bar; the
  header bolt shows the draw, lit while charging.
- **The bay in the world** (`machine_droneBay`, `BayInk`): a drone docks on an arm (`drone_dock`; `salvage_dock`
  higher, feet on the arm), the lugs (`droneBay_front`) over its feet, its eye unlit unless flat. Each arm's lamps by
  quarters of its drone's charge, the next blinking in while charging, the first red under a quarter, a little light
  pulsing while charging. The fuel slot: the canister in the tilted breech while it holds one, sliding in as one comes;
  its lamp red empty, amber holding one; for a second after one is spent the valve opens, the vane turns, the lamp
  blinks green. The dial's needle is fixed (too small to read). The beacon shines sky; without power lamps, fuel parts
  and beacon are dark. Its zone (`ZoneOf`) is outlined dashed sky over a faint fill while its frame is open; with a
  bay's card in hand every zone is, and the one the card would make, clipped at the grid.
- **Drones in flight:** `drone_fly_0..3` (rotor frames), lit (`PixelArt.Lit`), eye and lamp unlit over it. It follows a
  spring target (`Hover`, `DroneLook.Lively`) where the sim has it between ticks (`Trail`: its path averaged over
  `TrailTicks`, an even speed, corners curved, a little behind the sim). The target flies half a cell over the cell's
  centre, lower under a ceiling in the two cells above (0104); a doorway is no ceiling to a drone in one or flying up
  or down through it. It leans, faces its way, bobs, rotors turning. Cargo hangs from the clamp on a short tether,
  upright, swinging as a pendulum. Its lamp shines sky while its rotors run; flying home `Low` it blinks amber. Read
  every frame from the live list. No numbers, no seconds.
- **A salvage drone in flight** (`salvage_fly_*`, `SalvageLook.Drift`): no rotors, no wash; it bobs only in the
  ship's air (`AiredAt`), leaning at most 0.06 rad. Its nozzles puff against its acceleration (`Puffs`), the downward
  ones weaker in air, holding it up; a puff lives longer in air than in vacuum. Its net (`Grip`) is open, shutting round
  a load over 0.2 s; the piece's own picture (`DebrisLooks`, else the stack's icon) rests in it, swinging, the net's near
  side over it once shut. Its lamp shines sky, red blinking flying home `Low`.
- **Taking off and landing** (`Served.Changed`, every frame): a drone new to the list rises from the pad of its bay
  that just emptied, holding over it as its rotors spin up. One gone lands on the nearest pad of its bay that just
  filled: hovering, then down; the pad shows no docked drone till it touches down. One launched from a pad a drone is
  still landing on takes it over. One gone with no pad filled (dropped) is gone at once. A drone in the air when the view starts (a load, a join) is met where it is.
- **Heard** (`features/sound/README.md`): each drone's rotors where drawn, a loop spun up at lift-off and down on
  touching down or dropping, higher and louder faster, wavering, each tuned apart (`Whir`); a salvage drone's
  hiss while out, the jetpack's loop quieter (`SalvageThrust`, −14 dB, to tune by ear); a thud lifting off and
  touching down; a click as its clamp takes or puts a stack (`Served.Gripped`). On World, a station's reach.

## Deliberately not handled

- The bay and the badge's switch are silent; so is a drone flying home low: its amber lamp says it. A clamp putting
  cargo on the floor clicks as a delivery does.
- A drone flies in front of machines and heroes, never behind; tilted, its pixels are turned, not redrawn.
- A landing reads its pad from the slots alone: two drones of one bay docking one tick take the nearest pads in the
  order the view meets them.
- The badge on a machine with nothing to do yet still reads lit: drones would serve it.
- `Reached` floods every bay's zone ten times a second for the open frame: cheap at a few bays.
