# Audio credits

Every sound under `game/audio`: where it came from, who made it and its licence. CC0 only (ADR 0033):
the repository is public, so a file must be free to redistribute without attribution. A test
(`game.tests/SoundTests.cs`) fails on a file missing here or under another licence.

Kenney's files are converted to mono WAV and normalised to −3 dBFS peak; the room tone is cut to a
4.5 s loop, its tail crossfaded into its head, and encoded as OGG at about −20 LUFS. The generated ones
are placeholders made procedurally for Stillwake (band-passed noise, sine tones), the team's own work
released under CC0.
