Melee weapons

A pixel of art is a pixel of the world, 16 to a cell. Every held sprite lies the same way: the handle along +x from the grip, the head at the far end, the working side below (+y), so it leads the overhead swing. The dark wrap on each handle is where the fist closes. Numbers on the cards are concept numbers, not content.

  1. 1 · GripOn the handle where the fist closes, about a third up for axes and hammers, not at its end.
  2. 2 · Hold angleThe handle leaves the fist at an angle to the forearm, about 90° for axes, hammers and bars.
  3. 3 · Hand overThe front hand is drawn over the handle.
  4. 4 · Working partThe weapon marks its edge or head points; the hit zone is the path they sweep.

The poses use the game's hero parts and rig (Rig.cs): shoulder at 0.61 of the 42 px figure, arm segments 6.1 px. The grip sits at the fist's middle, 4.8 px from the elbow, a little short of the arm's end where the game hangs the axe today, so the fist closes over the handle. The weapon's angle is the forearm's minus the hold angle; the path runs from Raised through Strike to a short follow-through. Reach is how far the edge points get from the shoulder, scaled so the fire axe keeps its 2000. Files: held_*.png, icon_*.png, weapons.json.
