# The computer and the radar after a planet

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-08

## Context

The ship's computer and the radar antenna were built of what space gives alone, their blueprints understood once their
processor, display and transceiver were obtained: the last thing built before a planet. The user asked for both to come
later, after the first landing, made with magnetite and copper.

## Decision

- **Both machines take a planet's metals as they are built**: the computer ten copper and four magnetite, the antenna six
  copper and four magnetite, beside their parts. Research understands a blueprint once every part of its cost has been
  obtained, so both open only once a planet's copper and its deepest band's magnetite have been brought aboard.
- **Their parts stay of what space gives**: the drop pod takes a processor and a display, and it is how the first planet is
  reached; copper in those parts would lock the pod behind the planet it lands on.
- **The Ship tab follows the new order**: the launch platform, the drop pod, the computer, the antenna, then the engine.

## Consequences

- The space stage now ends with the launch platform and the drop pod; the radar comes with the engine, both of a planet,
  as the concept's first stage ends.
- We do not add a copper or magnetite part (a motor in the antenna's dish, say) because the metals in the cost say the same
  with no new craft.
