# A body holds its things

- Status: accepted; built
- Date: 2026-10-29
- Supersedes the drawing and taking back of a body in [0061](0061-death-leaves-a-body.md)

## Context

A hero's death laid every non-gear stack it carried on one point, and the client drew each one apart from the next, as it
draws stacks of different items resting in one cell: 1.2 cells a stack, a row 36 cells long from 30 stacks beside a
figure lying on its back. Taking them back was walking that whole row. The user: hide the things inside the body, or make
the body their store.

## Decision

- **A body's stacks lie inside it, unseen** (`FloorLayer`): only the figure and its beacon are drawn. The sim is unchanged:
  a body stays a tag on ordinary floor stacks (0061), and a death's stacks lie together, laid on one point, falling and
  drifting alike.
- **Touching it takes it all** (`Reach.PickUpsOf`): a hero within `Floor.BodyReach` (2) cells of where its stacks lie,
  some five cells long as it lies, sends `PickUp` for every one, as much as fits; what does not fit stays in it. One popup
  line for all it gave (`PickUpPopups.AddFromBody`), its things streaming out of its chest one after another
  (`PickUpFlights`), one pick-up sound. Anyone may take it, as before.
- **The pointer on it lists what it holds** (`Bodies.Contents`, `BodyView`): whose it is, the items the most first, up to
  eight and how many more, and that walking into it takes it all. Text on demand, by the pointer.
- **Gone, it crumbles** into its pixels as a killed creature does (`PixelBursts`, now in `game/shared`): emptied, or its
  time out. The figure's pictures keep their bytes on the CPU (`SuitArt.PixelsOf`); nothing is read back from the GPU.
  A surface left or a world loaded clears every body without a crumble.

## Deliberately not handled

- Choosing what to take out of a body: a panel listing its stacks could send the same `PickUp` per stack, client-only; left
  till play asks for it, most of all with a full bag.
- No magnet pulls a body; a body is walked to.
- A body expiring off the view crumbles there, unseen.
- Its stacks pushed apart (one pushed out of something built while another is not) would leave a stack outside the body,
  drawn nowhere; a death's stacks share one point and one motion, so no player action produces that.

## Consequences

`Floor.BodyReach` in the sim's content, read by the client only; `items.fromBody`, `bodies.*` keys. No save format bump.
