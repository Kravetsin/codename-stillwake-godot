# Kills burst into pixels

- Status: accepted; built; its burst superseded by [kills-crumble](2026-10-29-kills-crumble.md)
- Date: 2026-10-29
- Supersedes the death look of [0102](0102-creatures-live-in-the-sim.md) and the tick's cracks and plates

## Context

A kill ended in a 170 ms white fade, its loot laid still at the creature's top cell, taken only by walking onto it; every own
axe hit on a creature nudged the view. The user's word: killing should feel good, without screen shake, with one death for
every creature rather than one per species, the creature falling apart into its pixels; and a pick-up magnet, as stepping
onto every stack annoys.

## Decision

- **The hit-stop is the dying creature's alone:** held white `KillLooks.PopMs` (100 ms, two ticks), the world running on, for
  every weapon. No screen shake; the nudge on creature hits is gone (debris keeps its own).
- **Every creature bursts into its own pixels** (`PixelBursts`): read on the CPU from the pictures it is drawn with as posed
  (`CreatureParts.PixelsOf`, the tick's `Art.Albedo`), never back from the GPU; at most `PopPixels`, taken evenly. They fly
  out from its middle and away from the striker, carrying its speed, fall `KillLooks.Weight` times as hard as stacks, bounce off what is
  solid, land on it or on the pixels landed in their column, sliding a few down to a lower neighbour, and fade
  (`PixelDust`, one multimesh, lit). Afloat they drift as thrown. The hit, tufts and a puff are thrown as it bursts; the
  glint, shock ring and death sound as it is killed. The tick's sparks and flash stay; its plates are gone.
- **Loot sprays out of the kill** (`IItemsOps.Spray`): dealt a unit a piece, at most `Floor.SprayMost` pieces, every item at
  least one; each thrown away from the striker (a drawn side for a clinger lost with its wreck) at a speed and lift drawn
  from the world's Rng, afloat a share of them up or down. It exists from the kill's tick; the client draws and takes it only
  `PopTicks` later (`Reach.Hidden`), as the creature bursts.
- **Floor stacks land alive:** they spin in flight, squash and hop twice on landing with a clink, higher the fewer they hold;
  a rare drop (`KillLooks.Glints`) glints now and then.
- **The magnet:** a hero picks up any stack nobody dropped and that is no body within `Floor.PickUpReach` (2) cells of its
  body, flown home as a picked-up stack is; a stack let go for a friend, or a hero's body, only by walking onto it. The sim's
  `PickUp` has no range check: the client asks.

## Deliberately not handled

- Death animations per species, blood, corpses; freezing the effects for the hit-stop; holding the own swing.
- Glowing pixels: a glow channel per pixel is a second material path. Heroes disturbing heaps; heaps of two bursts meeting
  keep the higher column's top.
- Sprayed pieces fly two ticks unseen, so they appear a little ahead of the burst. A heap whose floor opens hangs till it
  fades. The magnet reaches through a one-cell wall. Every nobody's stack younger than two ticks is hidden, dug or felled
  loot too: a tenth of a second.
- Spraying loot from debris, harvests or digging: the spray's next users.

## Consequences

`Floor.SprayMost`, `SprayLeast`, `SprayFastest`, `SprayLiftLeast`, `SprayLiftMost`, `SpraySpaceShare` and `PickUpReach` in
the sim's content; `KillLooks` in the client's. No save format bump: the spray adds no field.
