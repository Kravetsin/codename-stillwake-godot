Played, the square was hard to see over a run of plans, and its tip over marks said one mark's refund. The aim's glow lay under
the plans' ghosts (`Layers.Aim` 11, the plans 19, the ghosts 0.88 opaque), so a twelfth of it showed through, and it adds light,
which a near-white ghost has no room for: no colour alone would have read.

**Over the plans, in teal.** The user picked on an Artifact, of seven colours and three draw orders: dark teal `#00b894`
(`Outline.SquareReady`), refused still `#e05555`, drawn at `Layers.SquareAim` (the plans' marks), over the ghosts, still adding
light. Only the square: one cell's aim keeps its blue under the plans, as the user picked the square's frame alone.

**The tip sums the marks.** With no blueprint in the square lacking anything, its tip adds up what every mark there gives back,
different walls and machines alike (`SquareTip`), under their name when all take down one thing.
