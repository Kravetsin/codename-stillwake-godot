# A bulb over the hero says something new waits on the board

- Status: superseded by [hologram-keys](2026-11-05-hologram-keys.md)
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Adds to [tablet-key](2026-10-31-tablet-key.md): its lamp stays as the lasting reminder

## Context

The lamp on the tablet's key was not seen in play: nothing moves there and the eye is on the hero. The user's idea was a bulb over the
hero's head, as if the hero had thought of something.

## Decision

- **A bulb, and it bursts.** The user picked from seven candidates on a page drawing each on the real hero: a plain bulb, one that bursts, an
  old cage lamp, the helmet's own lamp, a stylus stroke, a blank note and an electric arc. The plain bulb was liked and the cage lamp's end asked for, so the
  burst was drawn onto the bulb and that was the pick: the line draws on, the filament heats, overheats and stutters, a white flash, glass flying and falling with
  a few sparks, the cap with a stub of filament hanging a moment, a wisp of smoke, gone. About 2.5 s.
- **Once, and for this player only.** It plays when the number of unread notes rises with the tablet away, never for another player, whatever the crew found.
  Crew's finds are shared, so a friend's find plays it too.
- **Always one.** A find while it burns makes it burn on, 0.8 s from that find, the filament dipping and catching; a find after the burst adds nothing.
  Opening the tablet puts it out: what it said has been heard.
- **It does not rise for what waited already.** Opening a world with notes unread shows the key's lit lamp and no bulb.
- **The bulb goes with the hero, what it threw stays where it burst,** so a running hero leaves the glass behind. Afloat the glass drifts on and there is no smoke.
- **It keeps a size that reads.** The page drew at the camera's 2x, but the view a game opens on is about 0.7x at 720p, where the bulb was a dot and missed the
  whole point. So below a zoom of 1.4 it stops shrinking on screen; closer in it is the picture as picked.
- **Light and pictures:** a `Glow` lights the room, strongest at the flash. The pictures are exported by `tools/anime-art/subjects/bulb.js`, the page's drawing, with no
  watercolour finish: that is the look picked, and at this size the finish would not show.

## Deliberately not handled

- **Sound:** none yet. A new sound is its own pick.
- **An order met on the market:** it lights the key's lamp and no bulb. It is not a thought, and it is the player's own sale.
- **Glass and walls:** glass and sparks pass through walls and stop only at the height of the hero's feet, for under a second. A low ceiling is drawn over too.
- **A hero seated in a pod:** nothing shows, and the time still runs; the key's lamp says the rest.
- **Going to another surface as it plays:** what was thrown stays at the same pixels for the moment left. It takes leaving within a second of a burst.
- **Smoke in an airless room aboard:** smoke goes only with being afloat, not with the room's air. Few rooms have no air, and it is a wisp.
