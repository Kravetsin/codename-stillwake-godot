# The railgun traced from a picked reference

- Status: accepted; redraws the railgun of [the railgun](2026-10-27-the-railgun.md); drawn raw since [raw tool pictures](2026-11-14-raw-tool-pictures.md)
- Date: 2026-11-14

## Context

Weapons move one by one to pictures traced from a picked reference, as the [fire axe](2026-11-14-fire-axe-redrawn.md), the
[drill](2026-11-14-drill-redrawn.md), the [pickaxe and the harpoon](2026-11-14-pickaxe-and-harpoon-traced.md) did. Of five
references for the railgun the user picked «Катушечный из хлама», a home-made coil gun, over the capacitor cans it had.

## Decision

- **The picture**: a steel tube through five copper coils, cables looping from them to a green battery box at the rear, a
  rubber pad behind the box, a loop handle under it, an upright fore-grip under the tube. Traced whole (`refs/railgun.png`),
  40 × 15 pixels. The violet lamp on the box's front is too small for the trace and is drawn in `hero.js`.
- **Held by the loop under the battery**, its front leg as a pistol grip, in one hand as before; the fore-grip under the tube
  is drawn, not held. The tube from the battery to the muzzle is the edge the nail leaves by; the bench clamps the bare tube
  between the second and third coils.
- **Charged**, the coils glow cold blue with their windings still seen and a pale core, and the lamp burns: the same colours
  the charged rails had. The arc crackles over the coils, a box each third of them.

## Not handled

- The fore-grip invites a second hand, but the hero holds every gun in one; a two-handed hold would be its own change to the arms.
