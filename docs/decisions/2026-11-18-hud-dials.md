# The vitals as a row of dials over a long air bar

- Status: accepted; supersedes [hud-vitals](2026-11-13-hud-vitals.md), [the-thermometer](2026-11-14-the-thermometer.md)'s
  row and sign, and [0058](0058-one-place-for-every-machine-window.md)'s slot sizes
- Date: 2026-11-18

## Context

The body's temperature ([body-temperature](2026-11-18-body-temperature.md)) added a fourth row to the vitals: air, charge, the
thermometer and the meals stacked as strips of light grew tall and hard to read at a glance. The user wanted them compact and
the same in kind. Over pick pages on 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 and tries in the game, the user turned down:

- **hiding the calm gauges:** players learn where to look, and the air's segments show the gear worn;
- **vertical bars** ([Показатели стоймя](https://claude.ai/artifact/R6X3tC7QGGkzWDZQPCR99w)): the trouble was how many there
  were, not which way they ran;
- **empty meal rings or dots:** not compact;
- **air motion A–D** and the first looks, jerky and chopped: glow in stacked bands, hard cuts, steps once a second.

Two earlier rejections were reversed: rings round signs, and numbers on the HUD. The user called the charge's number "the most
precise".

## Decision

- **Picks:** the dials «A1» at a circle of 40 on [Шкалы кольцами: еда](https://claude.ai/artifact/CEvK4zreHp82jFptZJdUwG); the
  air's motion E, «как сейчас, но гладко», on [Дыхание шкалы](https://claude.ai/artifact/FYtG5JKMBNgkc7q43gzbx5); the
  thermometer «Ртуть» with G4, K1 and N3 on [Шкала термометра](https://claude.ai/artifact/7EgVeth9iMivKuw9xZGemn).
- **Place:** the block's foot 24 px above the screen's at the bottom-left, written in units of the base slot of 40 and grown by
  the slot (`Vitals.ScaleOf`): 1.1 at the normal slot, 1.5 at the large.
- **A row of equal dials** (`Vitals`): circles 40 across, a ring of light at radius 17.5 and 4 thick, icons 26, 16 between
  circles across and down. One ring everywhere: the vitals' strip of light bent round (`RingLight`), its glow on an added
  layer. A number sits under a dial that has one, in the mono, inked round. Read four times a second.
- **Charge, first:** the bolt in the circle, the ring cut into an arc per store on the belt in reverse slot order, each lit as
  far as it holds; under it the belt's charge in whole power units, thousands grouped, falling gliding (`EnergyCount`). Amber
  and pulsing while low, dim with none worn, the bolt and ring blinking red at a press the belt cannot pay; a store on hover.
- **Temperature, second:** the «Ртуть» glyph, a small upright thermometer with a test tube's bulb, in a two-sided ring: the
  normal at the top, the heat filling red clockwise and the cold blue counter-clockwise, both deaths meeting at the bottom; a
  notch a degree, a pointer at the normal. Glints run round toward where the place draws the body and stop at a tick (G4),
  red and quicker when that is past a death; flames lick the bulb in the heat and a crust of frost climbs it in the cold (K1);
  no number on the tube (N3). Under the dial the body to a tenth. Through the body's last breath the glass beats red with a
  heart. Hover: the body, «Здесь −160°», both limits, the crossed one red.
- **Meals, then:** a dial per meal eaten, 0 to 3, packed after the temperature, none for an empty slot. The dish's picture in a
  ring that empties back to the top as it runs out, both pulsing in its last minute; under it the time left, format A1:
  whole minutes rounded up («32м»), seconds in the last minute («45с»). Eaten, it pops in and the ring flashes; refused, red
  light and a jolt (every meal when full, its own when too soon); ended, it greys and fades and the row closes up.
- **The last dial:** the purge spot while a meal is held (held 3 s, a bile ring filling), else hunger's sign, its ring
  breathing ochre while hungry and dim while not.
- **Air, under the charge:** the oxygen sign in a circle of a dial's size, and the air's bar running right from it, a piece per
  part of the air (air-segments), about 6 units of length a unit of air until it would reach the tablet edge's keys, then
  shrunk alike to end 9 px short of their full row at every size (`Vitals.AirEnd`, `EdgeRow.RimLeft`): the bar never moves as
  keys come and go.
- **The air's motion, pick E** (`AirMotion`, `AirRow`): a shader lays the strip whole, longer pieces repeating its body
  (`StripCuts`), cut by a soft mask with round tips, its glow one smooth falloff. The lit edge follows the air on a critically
  damped spring (`CriticalSpring`) stepped with real time; a breath's change is spread over the breath; a hit, a canister or a
  part put on or off moves the aim at once and the spring eases it. The effects stay: red and pulsing low, the last units
  shimmering; a hit's lost air white, then drained; a refill's run and motes; a canister's white sweep; room air's wave; the
  visor fog dimming the last units.
- **The canister:** while it cools the oxygen sign greys and a ring sweeps round it; a canister asked for too soon blinks red
  only on the arc left to cool, and the sign.
- **Slots:** the normal slot is 44 px (40 before, 48 in a try), the large 60 (56 before); `Slots.Base` 40 is what the HUD's
  own sizes are written for: the vitals, the edge's keys, the markers and the hints grow by the slot over it.

## Consequences

- The block is two rows tall whatever is worn or eaten; the air's bar is the long thing on screen and the rest are circles.
- A meal's time and the charge are read as numbers; what a meal does, and each store, are still on hover.
- No food on the bars: their colour stays for damage, poison and danger.
- The frost star (`sign_cold`), the heat flame (`sign_heat`) and the meals' thin time lights are gone.

## Deliberately not handled

- The air has no number: its bar's segments and length say it.
- The dials do not hide when calm and do not reorder by urgency.
