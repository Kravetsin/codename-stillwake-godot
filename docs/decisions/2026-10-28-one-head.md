# One head for every hero

- Status: accepted; painted and drawn in the game (look 0 for everyone until the look editor, suits plan S-3)
- Date: 2026-10-28
- Builds on [0003](0003-character-rig.md); replaces the first trial of the bare head in `tools/hero-art`

## Context

The suits plan puts a person under the suit, whose look a player picks in the pod. The first trial head drew one eye
and five hairs inside the helmet and read as a lump. Five styles were tried on the concept page
[`tools/concepts/hero-styles`](../../tools/concepts/hero-styles): chibi, detailed, flat, retro and soft light. Style E,
soft light, was picked. Its first head was bigger than the helmet; two head shapes, man and woman, made more work for
the look editor than they gave, and Starbound tells its people apart by hair alone.

## Decision

- **One head for everyone**, inside the helmet's 13×12 outline on the neck's pivot. Heroes differ by hair, face, skin
  and hair colour.
- **At the body's pixel (1×).** A head at half pixels (2×) fitted a nose and lips into the helmet and turned more
  smoothly after the pointer, but mixed two pixel sizes on one figure; drawn from outlines it also looked worse than
  the 1× head drawn by hand.
- **The eye on row 6**, the head's middle and the middle of the visor's window, so a suited face looks out of the
  glass, not from under its rim.
- **Hair and beards have their own template**, 16×18 turning at (8.5, 14), bigger than the helmet, and are drawn only
  bare: never cut to fit a helmet. A glass helmet to come cuts the hair at its glass. The head and faces stay inside the
  helmet: `paint.js` fails otherwise.
- **A beard is its own pick**, not a face, so it goes with any face and hides under a helmet as hair does; brows and
  beards are in the hair's ramp, so they take the hair's colour.
- **Style E's light is painted in:** the outline only on the shaded back and underside, the lit front open, a cool rim
  (`R`) down the back of the hair and jumpsuit.

## Deliberately not handled

- **The rim shines in the dark too:** it is a colour, not a light. A shader could take it over if it jars.
- **A turned head steps** at 1×, as the helmet does today.
- **No head shapes, no body shapes:** a woman and a man read by hair and face only.

## Consequences

`tools/hero-art/parts.js` holds six hairs, five faces and two beards; the suits plan's look gains a beard (none, short,
full). S-2 draws hair and beard on their own pivot and leaves them off under a helmet.
