# Stamped and assembled; circuits found are rare

- Status: accepted; built
- Date: 2026-10-29
- Amends [the-workbench](2026-10-28-the-workbench.md) (which station makes what) and
  [0041](0041-wreckage-of-many-kinds-and-circuits.md) / [0056](0056-rich-finds-far-out.md) (how common found circuits are)

## Context

After the workbench the Former made the jetpack, the battery and the drill beside its parts: a press assembling tools
read wrong in play. Circuits were found too freely: a hero flying the far field met a cable rack every few seconds of
spawns, so the assembler mattered little. Making found circuits rare without care would lock the start: six machines
and the jetpack wanted one before any could be made, and the sentries that could drop them sit beyond the jetpack.

## Decision

- **The Former stamps:** parts, frames, motors, soil, canisters, the nozzle. **The workbench assembles:** besides the
  basic parts, every tool, the jetpack, the drill, the cape and the fuel pump. **The assembler** makes the battery
  with the other electronics. A frame in the jetpack and the drill still keeps them after the Former.
- **Only electronics hold circuits:** the Former, the crusher and the jetpack need none (the Former a coil, the crusher
  a tube, the jetpack two gears); the electric smelter and the assembler one each. Two found circuits close the loop.
- **Found circuits are rare:** cable racks a third as common in the stream and a quarter to a fifth in the far field;
  a sentry drops two or three, a fighter's share.

## Consequences

- The Former's inputs go back to three, a motor's.
- `EverythingButAPlanetsOwnOpens…` still reaches the drop pod: the jetpack, and with it the asteroid band and its
  quartz, needs no circuit.
