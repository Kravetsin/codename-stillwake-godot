# The rest of the art in one batch

- Status: accepted as a first pass; under `--anime-art` until the switch ([the plan](../plans/anime-art.md)).
- Follows [ink and watercolour](2026-11-01-ink-and-watercolour.md).

The user asked for every remaining picture to be drawn at once by parallel agents, in place of a PR per family and a page per look. So this
pass has no pick pages: the user judges contact sheets afterwards, family by family, and a family may be sent back whole. A picture drawn
here is not a pick; a look still wants the user's word before the switch.

What changed besides the pictures:

- **Past `HiRes`:** debris, far wrecks, the handbook, homes, the hero's worn and held pictures, the rod, the wrist and the tablet now go through
  it, off the flag as before. `WristArt.Slices` samples a 9-slice without mipmaps: a tiled stretch's UV jump would pick a coarse mip and draw a line at each repeat.
- **`LookTint`:** the hero's hair and skin pickers tint the ink pictures, or they would do nothing under the flag.
- **`PlanetSky`:** `sky` and `clouds` are far layers, read at any size. The sky is a slate wash, greyer than the pixels' blue.
- **The exporter:** it lets the browser choose its port, for two exports could meet on one and write each other's pictures, and it stops the browser's
  processes by profile, for they outlived a kill of the tree.

We do not draw `planet/{wall,back,top,detail}_*` (the atlas reads those names only as logical pictures), `wall-edge_*_0` (blank), `caves/vein*` (the
game does not read them), or new creature parts: the rigged creatures' pixel parts are replaced by [their ink designs](2026-11-01-creatures-in-ink.md).

Eight agents at once each running the game and a browser nearly took the user's machine down, so parallel art agents only draw: one process runs the game, after assembly.
