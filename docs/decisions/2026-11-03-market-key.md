# The black market has its own key on the tablet's edge, the tablet no tabs, the credits always in sight

- Status: accepted; the lens look, the saddle and the credit display superseded by [hologram-keys](2026-11-05-hologram-keys.md)
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Adds a sixth key to [edge-lenses](2026-11-03-edge-lenses.md)'s edge, beside the research key; drops the tablet's tabs from
  [tablet-tree](2026-10-30-tablet-tree.md); an order met lights the market's key, not [key-lens](2026-11-02-key-lens.md)'s; the credits leave the
  tablet's head for the edge, over [tablet-edge](2026-11-02-tablet-edge.md)'s charge

## Context

The market was a tab inside the tablet: T, then the market's tab, a press or two away from the board the tablet opens on. Players put off by
that, or not finding the tab, missed what the market sells, though it is where credits from salvage go, and it fills as orders are met.

## Decision

- **A key of its own**, right of the research key: a frosted lens as the others, its motif an onion: a bulb from a pointed neck, two inner layers
  paler. The user picked it from four on a page drawing each in every state at the game's size: a coin, a mask, an onion bulb and a hood; a larger
  onion, white, was tried after and turned down.
- **M and the market's key** take the tablet out on the market, go to it from the board, and put the tablet away from it. **T and the research key**
  do the same for the board. The edge is air, build, research, market, suit, journal: six, its metal widened to hold them.
- **No tabs:** with a key each, the board and the market are two pages of the tablet, with no strip over them: the market's own row of shelves is its top. The tablet
  always opens on the board by T, so the unread pile is met there as before.
- **Its lamp:** the market's key is lit while the market is out, and while an order met has not been seen on it. The research key lights only for
  unread notes, so each key tells of its own page.
- **The credits are always in sight:** on a dark display over the bezel's charge, the signal bars and battery drawn in the tablet's art, riding
  with the keys on the rim or whichever tablet is up: the coin and the number in gold. The market's "sort: type, then newest" line is gone; the
  order it names is kept.
- **The picture** is `key_market_<state>`, from `tools/anime-art/subjects/edgekeys.js`.

## Deliberately not handled

- **A lamp that tells an order met from the market being out:** both light the lens the same; with the market out its order is being seen.
- **The menu:** its Tablet choice still opens the board; the market is a key or M away.
- **The tablet's charge:** the art's battery is covered, not redrawn; nothing in the game runs it down, so it said nothing.
