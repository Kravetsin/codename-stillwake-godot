# The power driver's mode by the arrow

- Status: accepted; built
- Date: 2026-11-04
- Supersedes [pointer-looks](2026-11-04-pointer-looks.md).

Played, the builder's frame in place of the arrow was hard to read: snapped to cells, it made the mouse feel jerky. The user
took the pick page's other mark instead (https://claude.ai/artifact/3MgvejnNNhkfcW2LaFBGF2, "Знак у курсора"): the arrow stays,
and the mode shows beside it.

**The arrow stays** for builders and for a card in hand, as before pointer-looks; a carried stack still hides it, the inventory's
own. The one owner of the arrow went with its other users: one user needs none.

**The mark** (`ModeGlyph`, `DriverMode.DotsOf`): with the power driver in hand, over the world, its square's dots on a small dark
plate above and right of the arrow's tip, a dot a cell, the bottom row first. All lit (teal, `Outline.SquareReady`): the square.
The first alone: one cell. The square wanted with no charge: the first lit, the rest red outlines, waiting. A switch eases each
dot that changes over 160 ms, the far corner first. The screwdriver, of one cell, shows none.

**Above the tip, not below it** as on the page: the pointer's tips (a plan's cost) open below and right of it, where the mark
would hide under them.
