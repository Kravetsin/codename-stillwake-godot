# The drone's scan form is a reticle, its marks the gauge's dots

- Status: accepted; supersedes in part [drone-threads](2026-11-21-drone-threads.md) (the scan's spyglass and the gauge flying
  from its mouth)
- Date: 2026-11-21

## Context

The scan's spyglass ([drone-threads](2026-11-21-drone-threads.md)) was a picture of a tool, not of what the drone does, and
its 26 gauge dots came out of nowhere at its mouth. The lamp is a dish and a source; the scan wanted a shape of its own as plain.

## Decision

- **The reticle** «Прицел» (`tools/pick-kit/pages/scan-form.js`, candidate `reticle`, db doc `picks/scanForm`; the third round,
  over «Линза», «Стрела», «Штангенциркуль», «Рупор» and «Линейка», beside the spyglass and the lamp for reference): a ring round
  the core, four ticks that close in while the scanner reads, and 26 dial marks, a spark sitting on each.
- **Every spark takes part** by the pick's shares: 26 on the marks, 48 on the ring, 46 on the ticks, the ring and ticks flowing
  at their own paces. The polylines are `DroneInk.ScanMarks` and its neighbours, exported from
  `tools/anime-art/subjects/drone.js`.
- **The gauge's dots are the marks' sparks** (`ScanDots`): each leaves its mark for the row over the wreck, hops from wreck to
  wreck without coming back, and comes home to its mark whole when the pointer leaves; its mark dims while it is out. The row's
  rule is unchanged: dot j lit `clamp(left / (max / 26) − j, 0, 1)`, bursts on a hit, red with the alarm.
- The spyglass and its mouth are gone.

## Deliberately not handled

- A friend's drone shows the reticle while it scans, but its sparks never leave their marks: the gauge is this player's.
- No sound for the reticle closing in.
