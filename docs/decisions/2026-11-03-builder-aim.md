# One cell of work glows as the square does

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Partly supersedes [driver-square](2026-11-03-driver-square.md): "one cell's aim keeps its blue under the plans" no longer holds.

The user, once the square was teal: the faint one-cell aim and the screwdriver's name with the power driver in hand should have
been fixed with it.

**A plan or a mark aimed at glows teal over the plans**, at `Layers.SquareAim` in `Outline.SquareReady`, by the screwdriver or
the power driver in one-cell mode alike: one look for work on plans. Machines, doors, valves and a repair keep the blue under
them, at `Layers.Aim`, for over the plans their glow would cover the hero standing before them.

**The prompt names the builder in hand** (`Prompts.Of`'s `held`): `use.build.powerDriver` and its take-apart and repair, else
the screwdriver's words, which also tell a hand holding no builder what to take.
