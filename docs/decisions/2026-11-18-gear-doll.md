# The equipment is a doll under two tabs

- Status: accepted; amends [hero-skins](2026-11-16-hero-skins.md)'s Appearance key and the equipment rows of
  [0047](0047-equipment-the-jetpack-and-the-slower-swim.md) and [0058](0058-one-place-for-every-machine-window.md)
- Date: 2026-11-18

## Context

A coat-hanger key on the equipment's edge turned the same screen to the skins: same frame, same size, captions only. The
user often lost track of which one was open and tried to drag a suit into the skins. The gear itself was rows of slots
under small captions that nobody reads. Four live mock-ups went on a pick page: tabs alone, a doll, a stencil
silhouette, and no tabs with a skin chip on each slot. The user picked the doll.

## Decision

- **Two folder tabs** on the equipment's metal, Gear and Appearance (`GearTabs`). The open one is part of the frame, its
  name in the screen's colour. The Appearance screen's glass, glow and lamp turn warm amber (`WristScreen.Tint`). The
  hanger key is gone. With no Appearance set there are no tabs.
- **The doll.** The player's own hero stands in the middle, drawn live by crew's `HeroDoll`, unlit, dressed as in the
  world, skins included. Its dress is read after every read of the slots. The slots stand round it by what they dress,
  each with a leader line to its part (`DollLayout`, `DollLeads`), and have no captions; an empty slot's tip names its
  kind. Only the first belt slot has a line, lit while any belt slot is filled.
- **Appearance on the same doll.** Each skin slot stands where its gear does, the weapon effect by the hand.
- **A lifted stack turns Appearance back to Gear**, so nothing is dragged into the skins.
- **The screen ends past the last open belt slot.** Opening another widens it.

## Deliberately not handled

- No clothes slot yet. It comes with body-temperature phase 3 and goes right of the suit, widening the screen as a belt slot does.
- The doll never moves or turns; it stands as a picture of what is worn.
- The research board's "tools and gear" area is now «Инструменты и снаряжение», so the tab keeps «Снаряжение» to itself.
