# In a long flight the drone plays the tug

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-22

## Context

The user wanted a small detail for atmosphere, as the planet's leaves are: after some seconds of flight one way the drone
hooks a thread on its hero and they fly on tied.

## Decision

- **The pick** is «Буксир» after 2 s (`tools/pick-kit/pages/drone-tow.js`, candidate `tug`,
  [page](https://claude.ai/artifact/4eJaDUx33wYxw3Tb5HRqFp), db doc `picks/droneTow`), over five ways of being towed itself. It
  fits the drone being the stronger of the two.
- **`DroneTow`**, client only: a hero afloat at 0.8 of the jetpack's top or more, 2 s within 12 degrees of one way. The
  drone runs ahead round the head (behind the back when steep), throws a thread to the shoulder and heaves every 1.3 s,
  puffing sparks, squinting, glancing back.
- **It lets go** on a turn past 14 degrees, a brake, a wall on the thread's way, and for whatever matters more: a window, a
  dart, a visit, a gesture, an icon or lesson, an alarm, love, a meeting or seat, the hero passed out
  (`DroneIntent.Free`). After a turn, a brake or a wall it flies on alone and comes back round the head; for what
  matters more it goes at once.
- **Beside the pick:** the thread's end sits on the body as drawn, so a twist never leaves it hanging; a slanting flight
  pulls too, the thread clear of the glass; the eye alone glances back, the lamp and the reticle keep their aim.

## Deliberately not handled

- **The hero flies no faster.** A real pull would be a mechanic that pays for flying straight, not a detail.
- Each screen works out each drone's tow from what it sees: a friend's may hook a moment apart.
- A flight steered by a wandering pointer may never hook.
- The thread bends round nothing. No sound. Only afloat, so never on a planet or a floor.
