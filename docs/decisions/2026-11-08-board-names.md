# The board names what was never had

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-08
- Amends [board-thoughts](2026-11-02-board-thoughts.md) and [the pile](2026-11-02-board-pile.md): what was never had is no longer hidden

## Context

The board on the tablet (T) hid whatever the hero had never held: a shape's box said "2 more ?" with no picture, the strip
said "something not found yet" or "a machine not found yet", a part was a pale picture with no name, and a station not
yet had told nothing under the pointer. The user asked for every item and station on the tablet to be named, found or not.

## Decision

- **Everything on the board is named.** A shape's box shows what it lacks with its picture; the strip names every part,
  source, station and use; a part never had is written with its count, dimmed; a station none stands of is named by the
  pointer.
- What is pale still means what it meant: a part or station not had or not standing. Only the name stopped being withheld.

## Not changed

- The black market's locked goods stay ciphered: they are the market's to reveal, not the board's.
- The debris Handbook's "???" for finds none has come out of yet stays; it is not on the tablet.
