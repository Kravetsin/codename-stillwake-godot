# The drone scans each find over the hero's head

- Status: accepted; supersedes [edge-flash](2026-11-08-edge-flash.md); superseded in part by
  [form-vision](2026-11-22-form-vision.md) (a lesson showing its form by the head)
- Date: 2026-11-21

## Context

A find flashed along the tablet's edge into the research key ([edge-flash](2026-11-08-edge-flash.md)). With the drone the
hero's companion and the edge keys its own, the user wanted the drone to be what notices a find: the eye is on the hero, and
the drone is beside it.

## Decision

- **The beat** is the pick «Голограмма» made plain (`tools/pick-kit/pages/find-scan.js`, candidate `holo`, db doc
  `picks/findScan`), over «Контур», «Проекция» and «Очередь»: an item first held shows its plain icon over the hero's head, as
  the bag draws it; the drone's scan sweeps it top to bottom; it folds into one ember, and the ember flies to the «Изучения» key,
  which flares as it lands while its number takes the batch. 1.35 s from a find to the key (`FindScan`: `Lead`, `Rise`,
  `Sweep`, `Fold`, `Fly`, `Flare`).
- **A batch**: items had together come one after another, the next coming in as the one before folds; only the first record
  brings the batch onto the number. A batch with no item (a machine built, a blueprint) is an ember from the eye.
- **A lesson** ([drone-lessons](2026-11-21-drone-lessons.md)) plays first and longer: the drone goes to what it learned from
  (0.6 s: the nearest teaching machine, else the wreck last seen broken), fixes its eye on it and fills the scanner's 26 dots over
  it (3 s), shows the form by the head (1.5 s: the lamp lit, the scan out, the alarms pulsing red), then the form's thread icon
  comes in over the head as a find's and the key flares 1.2 s. Finds wait behind it; with nothing to visit the dots fill over
  the head.
- **Opening the tablet** puts out the finds, not the lessons; what waited when a world was opened plays nothing.
- **With no drone** a find lands on the key at once.
- Seen by this player only. `-- --find-scan` replaces `--flash` (`=unpaired` with no drone); `-- --lessons` plays a lesson.

## Deliberately not handled

- No sound for a find or a lesson.
- Which item it was shows only by its icon; the board says the rest.
- A friend's finds are never shown on this screen.
