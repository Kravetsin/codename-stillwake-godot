# The tablet's key is a frosted lens that lights

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Supersedes the glyph and lamp of [tablet-key](2026-10-31-tablet-key.md); the key's place, its press and when it is lit stay as they were

## Context

The key showed three cards and threads in the old pixels, with a small amber bar above them. It did not read as anything and was not seen in play. The user asked for a
bulb that lights when something is there, casting a soft light round it, as the inventory and the build tablet do round theirs.

## Decision

- **The whole cap is a frosted lens with a bulb's shade in it.** The user picked it from five candidates on a page drawing each on the real HUD: the head's bulb on the cap, a thin
  cut line, a glass bulb in a wire cage, the lens, and a bare filament. It replaces both the cards and the amber bar.
- **It lights from within** when the key was lit before: a note unread or an order met, with the tablet away. It comes on over 0.9 s and goes out over 0.6 s (`KeyGlow`).
- **A soft amber wash** spreads about 54 pixels from it, added over what is under it (`ScreenGlow`), so it shows over frost and space alike. It is a screen light, not a world's light.
- **Under the pointer** the lens is brighter, and cooler while dark. **Held,** it sinks a pixel and dims.
- **The pictures** are exported by `tools/anime-art/subjects/keylens.js`, the page's drawing, with no watercolour finish: at 22 pixels it would not show.

## Deliberately not handled

- **What can be bought:** it does not light for a good the player can afford. That would keep it lit most of the game and say nothing.
- **A sound as it comes on:** none. A new sound is its own pick.
- **The window's scale:** the lens is drawn at the key's size in screen pixels, as the other wrist keys are.
