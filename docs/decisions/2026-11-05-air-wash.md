# The air overlay is a wash to leave on

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

Most players turn the air overlay on (Q) only to see where they can breathe, and would leave it on all game. Its look was
still the old pixels' ([pixels unsupported](2026-11-03-pixels-unsupported.md)): an ordered dither in mint, a grid of grey dots
in every airless room, a ragged blue aura round the lifepod and louder motes. Too loud to leave on, and out of the ink and
watercolour the rest of the game is drawn in.

## Decision

- **The user's pick, A of five** on the air overlay page (a wash with a tide line; the others hatched the vacuum, frosted it,
  drew only the air's edge, or filled the air with drifting grain): where there is air, a faint cool wash (`WASH`, at about a
  tenth), granulated and pooled in two tones; along its wandering edge a dried tide line, twice as strong, so a filling room's
  front is a line crawling out of its vents. **An airless room shows nothing**: the wall as it is.
- **The paint moves, the air does not** (asked after the pick): the wash's blots and grain drift about 10 px a second one way
  (`DRIFT`; 2 px read as syrup, not air). The edge's wiggles move too, slower and each size its own way (`EDGE_DRIFT`,
  `EDGE_SWELL`, 2 to 4 px a second), so the tide line keeps changing shape: a still edge beside drifting paint looked pasted on. Where the
  air ends stays where the sim says, give or take the wiggles' few pixels.
- **Venting is the one alarm**: the wash turns rust (`RUST`) and rust streaks run through the air left toward the nearest
  opening. Once the air is gone the room shows nothing, though the sim still calls it venting while it is open: on the page
  the streaks stayed after the air, but a wreck's holed room would then be an alarm all game. The streaks' frame turns by
  sixteenths of a turn, so they never fan out where the headings toward a hole converge.
- **The lifepod's reach is air** with the same wash and tide line, its corners rounded, instead of an aura of its own.
- **Motes are as quiet with Q as without**: they no longer brighten under the overlay.
- Same inputs as before (`AirField`'s texel a cell, the pod's reach, `TIME`): only `AirLook`'s shader changed. Its hash takes no
  `sin`, so the grain holds far from the world's origin.

## Deliberately not here

- **No marking of the vacuum.** Air is what the player looks for; an airless room is the ship's plain wall, and its tip says why.
- **No light from the wash.** It stays unshaded and unlit, as the dither was: it shows the same in a dark room as in a lit one.
