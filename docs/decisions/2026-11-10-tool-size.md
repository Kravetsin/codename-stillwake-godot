# Held tools are drawn smaller, and reach no longer follows the model

- Status: accepted; supersedes in part [tool-reach](2026-11-05-tool-reach.md): nothing shows a tool's reach
- Date: 2026-11-10

## Context

The tools' pictures came from the pixel art, drawn for a larger hero. Beside the inked hero they looked bulky: the drill
nearly his size, the railgun over it. Fitting each weapon's range to its drawn tip was tried first (3.25 cells for the axe)
and was too short to dig with; at a true size made a third larger, small tools as the grinder shrank to a few pixels. The
fist also held every item by the glove's cuff, so a tool sat in the wrist, and the axe by the top of its wrap.

## Decision

- **Three quarters.** A tool or gun in the hand, on a bench and its rod in flight is drawn at `HeldLook.Drawn`, 0.75 of its
  picture, about its grip (`HeldLook.Size`, `RodLook.Size`). The user's pick in play, over the size as it was and true sizes.
  A look may take its own size later; the pictures are not redrawn.
- **The fist is the knuckles.** `Rig.FistBack` is half a pixel past the forearm's end, the glove's knuckles; the axe is
  gripped round its wrap at the haft's end, the drill round the middle of its pistol grip, not its back edge.
- **Reach is a rule of play.** Each weapon's range in `sim/Content/Weapons.cs` is set by how digging and fighting feel: the
  axe, pickaxe and sledgehammer 4.5 cells (the sledgehammer's down from 5.5), the grinder 5 (from 6.6) and the drill 5.5
  (from 8), a cell and a half past the wheel and the bit; the spear's 6, past every swung tool's. It is longer than the model, and nothing draws the difference:
  a swing's haze is the blade's own ([blade-trail](2026-11-10-blade-trail.md)), a thrust and a running drill show no air
  of their own, a running grinder only a haze round its wheel.

## Not handled

- The pictures are shrunk, not redrawn: their ink is a quarter thinner than the hero's. A tool that looks wrong so is redrawn.
- A hit landing past the drawn tip is accepted; if players find it unclear, the pointer can show whether a stroke reaches.
