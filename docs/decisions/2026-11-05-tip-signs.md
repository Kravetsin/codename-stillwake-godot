# Tips speak in signs and the bubble's numbers

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

The 2026-11-04 UI batch. The air overlay's tip showed over any cell of a room, machines included, and still spoke the old
model ([air-bubbles](2026-11-05-air-bubbles.md)): spans from the leak, "Vaporizer keeps up", and the whole network's making,
so two rooms on one vaporizer each claimed all of it. Tips spelled out O₂, leak and power in words, and no machine showed what
it draws before it ran: a station's panel read 0 at rest.

## Decision

- **The room tip shows only over a back wall or an empty cell of a room**, never over a structure or machine. Its name is the
  room's state and aired cells of its volume; then its **share** of its live vents' making (`RoomAir.Budget`, split as the sim
  splits it), and its aired cells' leak, a minute each, the leak red when the bubble stops short; the liquid behind its
  vaporizers and the sim's span, by the canister's icon, red at none, none for the pod; and why it falls short, red.
- **Rates read a minute**, to a tenth, rounded down, as the wall card already read: a vaporizer `+1200/min`, scrap `−3/min a cell`.
- **Signs, the user's picks** on the tip-icons page, screen glyphs of the bolt's family (`tools/anime-art`, `signs`):
  `o2.glyph` (an O₂ in sky, `sign_oxygen`), `leak.glyph` (a broken wall and an arrow out, `sign_leak`) and `power.bolt`
  (the bolt as it was). Each has a red twin for a line short of something (`…Short`, `sign_boltNone`). A tip line carries a
  sign or an item's icon (`TipLine.Sign`).
- **A machine's tip names it** and, under what its feature says, shows its power by the bolt whether it runs or not: what it
  draws with its modules, a maker's rated making after a plus. The build card shows the same; a station's panel shows its
  draw dim at rest, lit while it works.

## Consequences

- One vaporizer piped to two rooms shows each its half: the player sees the share, not the vaporizer.
- Machines that said nothing (crusher, lamp) now say their name and draw; a workbench, off the power, still says nothing.

## Deliberately not handled

- **Breathers** leave the forecast: the span is the sim's `Lasts`, which heroes breathing in the room shorten unseen. The
  client would repeat a sim rule, and the number would jump as heroes walk in and out.
- **Tanks shared by several rooms:** each room's span counts its own draw only, as the sim's `Lasts` does.
- **Rounding:** share and leak each round down, so a leak may read a tenth over a share it fits.
- Machine tips stay off under an overlay; pipes and cables do not hide the room tip.
