# The pixel art is removed

- Status: accepted.
- Follows [the pixel art is unsupported](2026-11-03-pixels-unsupported.md), whose pictures it takes out.

`game/art` holds only `hd/`, the ink pictures at four times the world's pixels. What needs a picture at the world's pixels, for a size or a shape, reduces its ink one (`HiRes.Logical`: solid where half covered, soft for a glow's weight); a picture missing there throws. The tools that drew the pixels are gone with them: `tools/ship-art`, `tools/planet-art`, `tools/homes-art`, `tools/hero-art` and the `tools/concepts` pages.

**What they made stays, as data.** `HomesArt.cs`, `RelicArt.cs` and the ruins' two sounds were generated by `homes-art`; they are kept as written and edited by hand. The ink export placed a few subjects by the pixel pictures' boxes (the cave crystals' base rows, the acacia's glowing pods): those numbers are frozen in `tools/anime-art/logical.json`. The title's plate keeps its box and its seat, lamp and ring (`PlateArt`), its pixels gone.

**Tests read the ink.** A test that compared a pixel picture now holds the ink one to the rule it stood for: the arc lamp's light round its glass, the bench's shard alight where its arcs leave, every machine but the hung lamps standing on its foot, the plate's points on its painted top.

## Deliberately not here

- **Measuring the sentries' perches again.** `Wreckage.Perches` were measured from the pixel pictures. The ink tops wander a pixel or two along them (an engine block's top is a row of pipes); `PerchesTests` holds each perch within two pixels of its picture's top over half its length, and the sim's numbers stay, so no save or hash moves.
- **A shader to repaint suits:** [the mark](2026-11-02-hero-clothes.md) replaces it.
- **Dropping `CreatureArt.Design`'s `Glow`, `Ramps` and `Ink`:** the ink export writes them; taking them out is a change to its subject, not worth one.
- **A style for each planet type beyond its palette,** till one is asked for. **Tuning for 4K.**
