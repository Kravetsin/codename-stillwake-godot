# Things are learned by having them, on the scavenger's board

- Status: accepted; built
- Date: 2026-11-05
- Supersedes the learning of [tech-rings](2026-10-30-tech-rings.md): gates paid by deposits, blueprints and crafts bought. Amends
  [black-market](2026-10-31-black-market.md), [room-upgrades](2026-10-30-room-upgrades.md),
  [first-hour-pace](2026-10-31-first-hour-pace.md), [tablet-tree](2026-10-30-tablet-tree.md) and [the-scoop](2026-10-31-the-scoop.md),
  each saying how in its status line

## Context

The first solo playtest of the rings was a grind: before the jetpack the gates took about 28 minutes of a workbench's hand work, and
every blueprint was bought on top. A castaway learns by what they lay hands on, not by paying for it. The user asked for an endless board
of notes on the tablet that writes itself as things are found, with credits buying only on a black market. Designed by `design-author`,
cut by `design-reviewer`, built in nine steps (`docs/plans/board.md` and `board-plan.md`, now deleted); what stays true is here.

## Decision

- **The rule.** A blueprint or craft is understood once every part's count (`Knowledge.Obtained`) is at `Understanding.Enough` and, for
  a craft, a machine that makes it stands on some surface. `Enough(item)` is `EnoughWorth` credits of the item's worth, rounded up, at
  most `EnoughMost` (200 and 10, `Content.Research`); a part worth nothing needs one. Today: scrap chunk, scrap, wire, planks, iron 10;
  gear, tube 8; hull plate, glass 5; plate 4; rotor 3; coil, canister, circuit 2; frame, solar cell, photosensitive panel 1 (`CountsTests`).
- **Known stays known.** `Research`'s walk grants into `Knowledge.Granted`, which is saved: a bench taken apart, everything sold or a full
  bag in space takes nothing back. The walk runs in derivation when `Knowledge.Version` or its surface's `grid.Version` has moved, and on a
  load's first tick; one pass, as the rule never reads `Granted`. What the market sells (`Content.Research.Market`) and upgrades stay outside it.
- **The wreck knows nothing from its start** (`StartKnown` is empty): the first walls at ten chunks. What the pod makes from nothing is
  understood on the first tick. A map with no `Layout.Known` knows everything, so most tests do not notice.
- **Obtained is counted in `Items`, where a stack is born**, on the roll, not on pick-up: seven sources would otherwise each call `Knowledge`,
  and the next would forget.

  | Counts | Op and callers |
  | --- | --- |
  | yes | `Output`, `Smelt`: a craft made, by hand or machine; a smelt's yields and what it draws besides |
  | yes | `GiveOrDrop`: a catch, taking apart, a dig, a felling |
  | yes | `GiveWhatFits`: a debris net collected, only what fitted ([debris-net](2026-11-03-debris-net.md)) |
  | yes | `Spray`: a kill's loot |
  | yes | `Caught`: a salvage drone's catch, when it catches, before it carries it home |
  | yes | `DropAt` for nobody: a wreck broken, a dig or a fall with no hero, a net's catch dropped with its net |
  | no | `DropAt` for a peer: a plan cancelled, the pod with nowhere to stand; paid before |
  | no | `Give`: a craft cancelled, pipe, valve and cable refunds, the kit; `LayDown`: the cost of a net a meteor breaks |
  | no | moves and spending: `Move`, `Drop`, `PickUp`, `Sort`, a death, `Take*`, `PutIn` (drones into crates), `Lift` |
  | no | `Spawn`, in creative |

- **Credits buy only on the black market,** the tablet's other page: room, and what cannot be made. Its later rings open by **orders**: a
  gate is done once `Knowledge.SoldOf` meets what it orders and its ring is reached, with no command. A sale is a capsule's launch, by hand
  or from an auto station, so an order takes nothing beyond what a sale takes. Sales count from the world's start. No deadlines, no penalties,
  and trust never falls; nothing on the main path is sold. A first cut, in `Content.Research.Tabs`:

  | Ring | Order | Opens |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 0 | none | `bag1`, `bag2`, `tradeSlots1` |
  | 1 | 40 scrap chunks, 20 iron, 20 planks | `bag3`, `bag4`, `tradeSlots2` |
  | 2 | 30 plates, 20 gears | `bag5`, `bag6`, `belt1`, power driver, `tradeSlots3`, `tradeSlots4` |
  | 3 | 20 glass, 20 silicon, 10 frames | `bag7`, `bag8`, `tradeSlots5`, heavy helmet |
  | 4 | 20 circuits | solar panel, `bag9`, `tradeSlots6`, `canister1`, salvage drone, plasma cutter |
  | 5 | 20 copper, 10 mica, 5 hide | `bag10`, `bag11`, `belt2`, regen suit |

  Its look is [market-grid](2026-11-03-market-grid.md)'s, its key [market-key](2026-11-03-market-key.md)'s and [hologram-keys](2026-11-05-hologram-keys.md)'s.
- **The board** (client, `game/features/research`). A note is a thing obtained or known, in ink; a silhouette the pale shape of a recipe not
  known, its pencil the mean of `min(count, enough) / enough` over its parts, a standing station one more part for a craft. A note runs at
  most 3 threads (`BoardThreads.Most`), to the shapes closest to done, ties in content order; a shape with every part noted and no thread
  hangs one from the part noted last. A shape is only ever a direct use of something noted; the market's goods show none until bought.
- **Slots** are a pure function of `Obtained` in its order and the recipes (`BoardSlots`), so a late guest gets the same board. The first
  note stands in the middle. A thing takes its slot when its first part is noted, or when it is itself obtained if sooner: the next free
  slot on an arc round that part. Nothing moves on its own: a burst of five is five shapes inking in where they stood. What is known with no
  part noted stands in a row above the first note. Edges, the camera's floor and the player's room: [board-edges](2026-11-02-board-edges.md),
  [board-room](2026-11-02-board-room.md); new finds as a pile: [board-pile](2026-11-02-board-pile.md); what a shape lacks and how long a mark
  lasts: [board-thoughts](2026-11-02-board-thoughts.md); the key and the bulb: [hologram-keys](2026-11-05-hologram-keys.md).
- **The world id** keys a board's file, as the seed cannot: every new world is `World.Create(1, …)`. `World.Id` is a random non-zero number
  the client draws for a new world, written at the save's root when not 0 and never read by the sim; a guest has it from the Welcome save,
  so the protocol is unchanged. `user://boards/<id>.json` keeps only what this machine's player moved and read, never sent: knowledge is
  shared, a layout is not. Id 0, old saves and dev worlds, keeps no file.

## Consequences

- Format 43 dropped the gates' deposits and nothing else: `Granted`, credits and upgrades stayed, and an old world's counts began at none.
- `CountsTests`, `UnderstandingTests`, `MarketOrdersTests` and `FirstMinutesTests` hold it; the former and the burner are understood within
  540 s of the bench (`ContentTests.Production`), where the rings' press and machinery gates were 21 minutes of it.
- The lamp (2 wire, 2 scrap) is understood long before anything makes power, which [the-scoop](2026-10-31-the-scoop.md) had avoided.

## Deliberately not handled

- **Build and take apart counts as taken apart:** we keep no record of what was built, and it only helps by minutes.
- **Loot that drifts off counts:** it was seen coming out.
- **The board does not state the goal:** nothing says an engine and a radar end the stage; a player finds them by following what they make.
- **Who the mafia is:** no characters, no text but its orders. **Orders are written by hand**, a small chosen catalogue.
- **A later ring may open at once** on what was sold before: sales are not counted per ring.
- **Heroes do not see each other's boards;** no note is shared, pointed at or written on. No search, no undo of a drag, no reset of a layout.
- **Notes for upgrades:** none; they are the market's tiles.
- **Creative and the testbed show a blank board,** as `Spawn` does not count. **A content update can shift notes nobody moved,** as slots
  follow the recipes. **A known thing in an old world's row may move** when its first part is noted.

## Risks

- **Flares arm early and unchosen.** `MagneticFlares.ArmedBy` is the oxygen `generator`, understood with 3 rotors, 8 tubes, 10 wire and a
  frame, which the first minutes can give. Flares need a cabled machine and repair is free, so it is pressure, not a softlock.
- **The ship pays too well:** door segments give plates and gears in seconds and their own blueprint; the player pays with a lock.
- **Credits run short of uses** until contraband: the goods added since (power driver, heavy helmet, salvage drone, plasma cutter, regen
  suit) eased it.
- **An order may pull scarce goods from the main path:** rings 3 and 4 want silicon and circuits, where quartz is the bottleneck.
- **The grind may only have moved:** 540 s of bench is arithmetic, not timed.

## For the playtest

- Are 3 threads a note enough for a board that invites wandering?
- The orders and what each ring opens, a first cut.
- Does a player find the goal, the engine and the radar, by following what they make?
