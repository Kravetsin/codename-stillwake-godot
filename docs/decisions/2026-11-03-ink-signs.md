# Ink signs: the bolt, the credit chip and the air lamps

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-03

## Context

After [no more pixels](2026-11-03-pixels-unsupported.md), the interface still had pixel pictures from `AsciiArt` in plain sight. The
power bolt stood before every power number and over machines short of power, beside the wrench and the loose plug. The coin
stood before every amount of credits. The suit gauge under the hotbar was a row of flat boxes. Item cards also wrote a sale
price as «Продается за N», unlike every other price. The user picked each look on its own page:

- the power sign, on [Знак энергии](https://claude.ai/artifact/79LSqYKFb4Ddr2A9i1scAw): «Экранный знак»;
- the credits, on [Значок кредитов](https://claude.ai/artifact/CgyawnaveLM19hfEQjFqhv): «Кредитный чип»;
- the air gauge, on [Полоска воздуха](https://claude.ai/artifact/9YoZ2MBnPCeeJoVnHuH5cH): «Рубленые лампы».

## Decision

- **The signs are glyphs a screen lights.** The bolt, the red bolt for no power at all, the loose plug and the wrench in its
  orange frame have a hard core, a body, a rim and a halo in steps, with scan lines every fourth row. The `signs` subject
  draws them, and `Signs` hands them out.
- **Credits are a chip.** It is a slab of gunmetal on a diagonal with brass teeth and an amber lamp, drawn by the `credit`
  subject. `CreditLine` writes every amount with it, so the market, the trade station and item cards all show the chip and
  the number.
- **A sale price is written like every other price:** the chip and the number, a little larger, with no words. The
  `card.sells` key is gone.
- **The air gauge is a row of hand-cut lamps.** Each lamp is a leaning box with one corner cut, an ink hairline and light
  in hard steps. The `gauge` subject draws five shapes in four tones (unlit, sky, red, pale), plus a wider set for the
  large slots. `WristGauge` lays one lamp in each cell and cuts the lit lamp at the fill pixel by pixel. Low, the lamps turn
  red. The sweep, the surge, the wave and the lost part are the pale lamp drawn over the lit one.
- What the page showed and the game leaves out:
  - **No glow halo round the lit lamps.** Under the hotbar, it blurred the lamps into one bar.
  - **The wave is drawn in two hard steps, not a soft bell.** The lamps stay hand-cut even while the light runs over them.

## Consequences

- `AsciiArt` keeps only the plug, the capsule, the book, the crack and the peg. Those are still pixel pictures.
- `--signs` shows the testbed with every power maker broken. It puts a wrench over each maker and a red bolt over everything
  that draws power.
