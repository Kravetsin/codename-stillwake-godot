# The hero's gait

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-04
- Supersedes: in [0036](0036-upright-in-space.md), the free hands paddling afloat

## Context

The hero is a beaten scavenger blown off a ship, and the playtest found its motion too brisk, with flaws on top. The
boots sank through the floor: the shin and boot were one rigid picture, so a leg swung forward tipped its toe under the
floor, and the body was set on its feet before it leaned, so a lean put one foot in the floor and lifted the other. In a
run the near arm swung forward with the near leg. A walk turned into a run at once, and from standing the stride began
at full swing. Afloat, the hands paddled in circles, which read as broken arms.

New poses drawn from scratch (a crouched, tired gait, arms placed by reach) were rejected outright; the current poses
stay, and only these flaws are fixed.

## Decision

- **The boot turns at its ankle.** The shin's picture is cut at the ankle (`Rig.Sole` over the sole); the boot below
  turns on its own node, its picture reaching a pixel up under the shin so no gap opens. On the floor each boot turns to
  lie flat, within `HeroMotion.Feet.Up` and `Down` of its shin's line; past that it rolls onto its toe or heel. A boot off
  the floor goes back to its shin's line as it rises, over `Feet.Lifting` pixels.
- **The body stands on its soles, after it leans.** `Poses.Grounded` lowers the body, turned as it leans about its
  middle, until its lower sole's lowest corner is on the floor. As the figure settles the limbs the body keeps its own
  height, and a boot it would put through the floor turns flatter, then its knee bends (`Poses.Kept`): set again on the
  lower sole of legs lagging a run, the drawn body jumped up to two pixels a frame though the pose rode smooth, and that,
  not the camera, shook the screen; lifted out of the floor instead, it still jumped half a pixel as quick feet came down.
  A boot under the floor turns flatter before a knee bends in the pose too: nearly straight, a knee folds half a radian to
  lift its ankle half a pixel, and pushing off it buckled for a frame.
- **A landing reaches the ankles, on flat boots.** The knees bend so the hips come down by the sink. Folded deep, a shin
  under the hip leans back further than an ankle turns, and its boot rolled onto its toe, so the hero seemed to land on
  tiptoe: the feet go further ahead instead, so each shin stands within `Feet.Up` (less a spare) of upright. Falling, the
  boots turn flat for the floor as the fall's pose comes in; rising, they still point along the shins.
- **The run rides smoothly.** At a full run the legs go through a stride in about a third of a second, a frame each
  twentieth of it: a body set on its lower sole each frame jumped a pixel or two a frame, its flight lasting a frame or two,
  and the step on the near leg, its hip lower as the body leans, sat a pixel under the far one's. The hips now stay level as the
  body leans (`Rig.HipRise`), and the body rides a smooth curve: lowest, `Run.Give` under where the leg on the floor stands
  upright, and `Run.Bounce` higher as it leaves the floor: up over `Run.Rise` of the step, down quicker, the weight
  landing; rising and falling alike it bobbed like a ball. A leg whose boot would go through the floor bends at the knee until it
  stands on it, hardly: riding lower than a step stands, the knees folded over a radian, a run in a crouch. Into a run the
  bent knees blend in with the gait.
- **A running foot stays where it came down.** The leg swept back at half the body's speed, so a foot on the floor slid
  twenty pixels on and the hero skated, pushing nothing. Now a foot comes down a few pixels ahead of the hips and the leg
  sweeps back as fast as the body goes on, for a fifth of a stride, as a sprinter's short, quick step: then the foot is
  well behind, the leg out straight, and the rest is flight. Off the floor the thigh still goes back a little, the heel
  kicks up quickly so the toe never drags, and before the foot comes down it swings back to meet the floor going by.
- **The body works with the legs.** Leaning alike all along, the body rode its legs like a plank. It pitches `Run.Pitch`
  further in as a foot pushes off and as far back up in the flight; the legs turn back by as much, so only the body
  pitches. The arms pump `Run.Pump` (0.8), the elbows bending `Run.Elbow` more forward and opening as much back.
- **Arms against legs in a run.** The run's legs are `RunLead` (0.6 of a stride) ahead of the arms, so the near arm is
  furthest forward as the near leg pushes off behind, as the walk has it. The legs lean with the body, the body ahead of
  the leg pushing off: swung back toward upright under a leaning body, they ran in a crouch.
- **The gait is eased.** The figure keeps the legs' cycle (strides gone through) and blends at one cycle from
  backpedalling through walking into running over `Gait.RunMs`, and from standing into a stride over `Gait.StartMs`,
  back over `Gait.StopMs`. Off the floor the gait is what the speed says, so a landing runs on at once. Only the drawing
  eases: the controller answers as fast as before, and steps sound by the cycle.
- **Afloat the hands are calm.** The suit moves the hero, not its hands: they hang by the body as when drifting still,
  trailing `Adrift.Trail` (`TrailFast` fast) back of the way it goes. The legs still kick.

## Decided

1. The real acceleration stays (a full run in about 120 ms): slowing it changes the game, not its look.
2. The boot picture is not redrawn: the shin's picture is cut at the ankle row every shin shares.
3. A run bobs 0.9 px. With hardly any flight more read as the hero being tossed up, and 0.6 px once the feet planted
   still lacked a push; more than 0.9 moves the body further a frame than a smooth ride does.
4. A deep landing puts the feet a few pixels ahead of the hips, sitting back over them, for its fraction of a second.
5. The body does not surge forward and back along the way: the pitch carries the shoulders a pixel ahead on each push,
   and a surge needs the figure drawn off the hero's own place.

## Consequences

- At a deep landing, a 1 px seam can show where the boot meets the shin.
- Another's hero eases its gait from its sent motion, as its other poses: friends see it alike.
