# The hero's gait

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-04
- Supersedes: in [0036](0036-upright-in-space.md), the free hands paddling afloat

## Context

The hero is a beaten scavenger blown off a ship, and the playtest found its motion too brisk, with flaws on top. The
boots sank through the floor: the shin and boot were one rigid picture, so a leg swung forward tipped its toe under the
floor, and the body was set on its feet before it leaned, so a lean put one foot in the floor and lifted the other. In a
run the near arm swung forward with the near leg. A walk turned into a run at once, and from standing the stride began
at full swing. Afloat, the hands paddled in circles, which read as broken arms.

New poses drawn from scratch (a crouched, tired gait, arms placed by reach) were rejected outright; the current poses
stay, and only these flaws are fixed.

## Decision

- **The boot turns at its ankle.** The shin's picture is cut at the ankle (`Rig.Sole` over the sole); the boot below
  turns on its own node, its picture reaching a pixel up under the shin so no gap opens. On the floor each boot turns to
  lie flat, within `HeroMotion.Feet.Up` and `Down` of its shin's line; past that it rolls onto its toe or heel. A boot off
  the floor goes back to its shin's line as it rises, over `Feet.Lifting` pixels.
- **The body stands on its soles, after it leans.** `Poses.Grounded` lowers the body, turned as it leans about its
  middle, until its lower sole's lowest corner is on the floor. The figure grounds the limbs as drawn, after they settle,
  so easing never sinks a boot; a run's flight is added over that.
- **A landing reaches the ankles.** The knees bend so the hips come down by the sink; past how far an ankle turns the
  boots roll onto their toes and the ankles come lower, so the hips sink as far.
- **Arms against legs in a run.** The run's legs are `RunLead` (0.7 of a stride) ahead of the arms, so the near arm is
  furthest forward as the near leg pushes off behind, as the walk has it. The arms pump less (`Run.Pump` 0.65). The legs
  swing half the run's lean back toward upright (`LegsUpright`), so a foot lands nearer under the body while the whole
  body still leans in.
- **The gait is eased.** The figure keeps the legs' cycle (strides gone through) and blends at one cycle from
  backpedalling through walking into running over `Gait.RunMs`, and from standing into a stride over `Gait.StartMs`,
  back over `Gait.StopMs`. Off the floor the gait is what the speed says, so a landing runs on at once. Only the drawing
  eases: the controller answers as fast as before, and steps sound by the cycle.
- **Afloat the hands are calm.** The suit moves the hero, not its hands: they hang by the body as when drifting still,
  trailing `Adrift.Trail` (`TrailFast` fast) back of the way it goes. The legs still kick.

## Decided

1. The real acceleration stays (a full run in about 120 ms): slowing it changes the game, not its look.
2. The boot picture is not redrawn: the shin's picture is cut at the ankle row every shin shares.
3. A run's body sinks more at a push-off than before, about 3.2 px: before, the foot floated there instead.

## Consequences

- At a deep landing, a 1 px seam can show where the boot meets the shin.
- Another's hero eases its gait from its sent motion, as its other poses: friends see it alike.
