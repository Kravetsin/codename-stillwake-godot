# A running tool hits with what is drawn

- Status: accepted; supersedes, for the drill, grinder and plasma cutter, the fan of [0077](0077-a-planets-ground-grows-from-its-seed-and-is-dug-by-hits.md) and the contact of [tool-contact](2026-11-05-tool-contact.md)
- Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

A running tool hit with a fan of rays from the hand and a wreck's whole box, out to the weapon's `Range`, while its picture was
a bit or a disc a few cells long. So the stream of sparks jumped to a wreck the tool did not touch: it "magnetised". The player
could not tell from the picture how far the tool reached, and no overlay fixed it: the user turned down every reach cue tried,
from rings to a glow at the pointer, as noise or as dim where players aim roughly.

## Decision

- **The hitbox is the drawn edge.** Each tool's working part as the figure draws it this frame (`HeldLook.EdgeFrom`→`EdgeTo`),
  `Bite` either side: the drill's bit; the grinder's disc, the half ahead of its hub (`Round`); the cutter's beam, from its
  nozzle along the barrel to `Range` from the hand, stopped at the first wall, cell that stops a body, or wreck pixel. A drill's
  or grinder's edge is never stopped (`ToolEdges.Hits`, sampled every half pixel).
- **Everything it touches is hit**, once a hit: every cell under it, every wreck in space whose opaque pixels it overlaps as drawn
  (`WreckPixels`; a picture that will not load counts as its box), every creature whose body it overlaps.
- **The client decides, the sim trusts it** (the user: "not competitive, the player's feel first", principle 6). One paced hit
  sends `HewCells(Tool, Cells)`, `HewPieces(Tool, Pieces)` and a `Strike` per creature, each only if the sim would take it. The
  sim checks no reach and no position: each listed cell is struck as a ray takes it, each listed wreck in space once, ascending,
  duplicates and gone ones skipped; the refusals are those of `Hew` and `HewWrecks`. A hit consumes nothing, so three commands
  cost what one did.
- **Seen where it first touches**: the sparks and the jet's splash at the touched point nearest the tool's base; touching
  nothing, the beam at its full reach. A friend's from its own figure.

## Consequences

- The drill and grinder reach only as far as drawn, about 3 cells less than before; `Range` now matters only for the cutter.
- A wreck is outlined only while the edge touches it, red when the sim would refuse the hit; cells and plants get no outline.
- Melee weapons keep their fan and `Hew`/`HewWrecks`; they move onto edges next, with a swept edge.
- Deliberately not handled: a flick of the mouse or a slow frame between two hits sweeps the edge past a wreck unhit; a modified
  client hits anything anywhere; a wreck's rocking is left out of its pixels; two screens may put a friend's sparks a pixel apart.
