# An aimed item comes up only in use

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

A held drill, grinder, cutter, gun, harpoon or spear pointed at the pointer every frame. Swimming away from the pointer, the
hero faced where it swam while its arm reached back through its body to the pointer (the spear: its thrust did not keep the
hero facing). The user, after Terraria: only the head follows the pointer; the item points there only while the left button is
held. Aiming before a shot helps nothing, as the player aims with the pointer anyway.

## Decision

- **Up only in use** (`ArmUp`): an aimed item's arm comes up while it is used, the left button held or clicked, the drill
  running, the harpoon's rod out, the spear thrust, and stays up `ArmUp.LingerMs` after, so a shot let go at the release
  (the railgun's) leaves along the aim and quick clicks keep it raised. Otherwise it hangs in the carry pose; the head looks at
  the pointer as before.
- **Up, the hero faces the pointer**, swimming the other way too (`HeroInput.Swings`); aboard it faces the pointer as before.
- **Shots and hits wait for the arm**: it comes up over `ArmUp.RaiseMs`, quicker than a swing's hand-over, and the figure says
  when it is drawn aimed (`Figure.Aimed`). A click before then waits for it, never fired from the hanging barrel; a drill's edge
  hits nothing till then (tool-edge: the drawn edge hits).
- A hanging drill aims at nothing: no outline, no prompt, until its edge is up.

## Consequences

- A first shot leaves about `RaiseMs` after its click.
- Friends' items already hung unless their drill ran or their rod was out; their guns still hang, their aim unsent.
- Deliberately not handled: the drill's motor runs from the press while its arm comes up, hitting nothing for those few frames.
